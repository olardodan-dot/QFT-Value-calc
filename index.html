Model notes

This draft uses the six state profiles and the labor lookup values saved in the source workbook. Screening outcomes are modeled estimates. TB treatment costs are excluded. Confirm pricing, reimbursement and labor assumptions before sharing an estimate.

By default, the X-ray setting applies to both cost and profit calculations. QFT reimbursement is based on the applicable patient claim type, with cash collected separately. Excel rules mode restores the workbook’s X-ray behavior and its single-rate reimbursement formula for comparison.

Contract uses the price you enter. Non-contract uses the Average QFT Pricing column in ContractPricingTable, based on annual QFT test volume. Public Health and Government start at the selected year’s table price and allow a scenario override.

State defaults apply when you select a state. State defaults apply specified positive-result rates to the number of people screened and use the Excel formulas where a rate is unspecified. Excel formulas mode uses modeled positive results for both tests. Rhode Island uses its saved population inputs with the same formulas. Rhode Island timing uses the supplied visit and follow-up values.

Source workbook: QFT-TST-Value Calculator-Multi-Center.xlsm. This version covers the workbook’s Clinic and Hospital TST workflows. Excel parity with populated scenarios is pending.

Start Here / FAQ

Which tab should I use?

Use Calculator for a custom scenario in one state. Use Sensitivity Analysis for the six-state study at 1,000, 1,500, or 2,000 new hires per hospital. Enter custom study prices once and they carry across the three study tabs. Calculator inputs are separate.

What does the study assume?

Each scenario models a hospital with in-house QFT processing and blood draw, two-step TST, and new hires only. Study base prices are $29.43 per QFT test and $12.50 per TST. The study uses state-specific X-ray costs and TST non-return rates.

Why did savings change when I switched positivity settings?

State Defaults (Survey Input) uses a survey rate where supplied and the Excel formula elsewhere. For Rhode Island at 1,000 hires, the survey TST rate yields 50 estimated positives versus 71 with Excel. Fewer follow-ups lower TST costs, so QFT savings are smaller. At base prices QFT still costs less under both settings.

When does reimbursement count?

Only the Patients group generates modeled reimbursement. New hires and current employees generate $0 even when a reimbursement rate is visible. Eligible QFT claims depend on the processing and blood-draw settings. The Advanced Excel validation switches reproduce legacy workbook behavior. Leave them unchecked for normal use.

How do I reproduce a checked result?

In Calculator, select Oregon → Hospital → Send out → In-house blood draw. Enter 1,000 patients and 1,000 new hires, leave current employees at zero, and keep two-step TST enabled. Use Contract pricing with $30 QFT and $15 TST. Select Excel formulas for positive-result rates, keep in-house X-rays enabled, and leave both Advanced switches unchecked. Use $5.00 QFT draw reimbursement and $10.82 TST reimbursement.

Under Patient reimbursement, expect $5,045 for QFT and $10,820 for TST. Set Patients to zero and both become $0.
